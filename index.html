About

About Dreadvane

Dreadvane is a heavy-metal project built around raw early-1980s energy, twin-guitar attack, galloping bass and direct songwriting. The music favors weight, tension and clarity over polish.

The songs

The songs focus on people caught inside historical, military and industrial events — less fantasy, more consequence. The aim is heavy metal with atmosphere, urgency and a strong human center.
